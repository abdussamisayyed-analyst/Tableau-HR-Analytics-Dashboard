# HR Analytics Dashboard | Tableau

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed in Tableau to analyze workforce demographics, employee performance, hiring trends, attrition, salary distribution, education levels, and department-wise employee insights.

The dashboard helps HR teams and business stakeholders make data-driven decisions by providing a comprehensive view of employee data through interactive visualizations and filters.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall workforce health
- Analyze employee hiring and termination trends
- Understand department-wise workforce distribution
- Evaluate education and performance relationships
- Track salary patterns across demographics
- Analyze employee age distribution
- Provide detailed employee-level insights

---

## Dashboard 1: HR Dashboard Overview

### Key KPIs

- Total Hired Employees
- Active Employees
- Average Tenure (Years)
- Terminated Employees

### Interactive Filters

- Department
- Job Title
- City
- State
- Year of Hire

### Visualizations

#### Hiring vs Termination Trend
Tracks employee hiring and termination trends over time.

#### Employees by Department and Job Title
Shows workforce distribution across departments and job roles.

#### Employee Distribution by City
Geographical analysis of employee locations across India.

#### Gender Ratio
Displays workforce gender distribution.

## Key Insights

- Workforce is concentrated in specific departments and job roles.
- Bachelor's degree holders represent the largest employee segment.
- Majority of employees belong to the 25-54 age group.
- Salary patterns vary significantly across education levels.
- Hiring trends have remained relatively stable over the years.
- Geographic distribution highlights workforce concentration across major cities.
